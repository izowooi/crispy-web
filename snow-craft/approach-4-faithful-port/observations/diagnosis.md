2. Drove the page with `playwright-core` (`/tmp/diagnose2.mjs`):

   - Clicked Start at canvas-space (296, 238).
   - Waited up to 15 s for the three reds to walk to their anchors (`r.walking === false`).
   - Parked the cursor at `(10, 10)` (off-canvas) and screenshot the canvas (`03_circle_a_settled_no_hover.png`, `03_circle_c_off.png`).

   - Press-and-hold on red 0; screenshot at `t = 0/250/500/750/1000 ms` (`04_meter_t*.png`).
   - Released; screenshot final (`99_final.png`).
